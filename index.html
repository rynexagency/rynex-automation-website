The Customer Called. Nobody Picked Up. They Ordered Elsewhere.

This happens in every restaurant without a system. Calls get missed. WhatsApp goes unanswered. Staff are busy with tables. We fixed that. Now every call, message, and booking enters one system — and the right response goes out automatically.

Main Entry Point

Calls, forms, and WhatsApp messages enter one place. The system reads the intent and triggers the right workflow immediately.

The Brain

Logic sorts every request — booking, order, support, or event enquiry. No manual reading. No missed routing.

Operational Flows
  • Smart Reservations: Accepts bookings, handles changes, and sends confirmations — no staff needed.
  • Order Follow-Up: Captures the request, confirms status, and updates the customer automatically.
  • Catering Enquiries: Collects event details and alerts your team with everything in one message.
  • Auto Logging: Every single enquiry is saved — ready for follow-up or monthly review.
System Highlights

Faster first replies. Fewer dropped requests. Cleaner team handoffs. A customer experience that runs reliably — from first message to final action.
